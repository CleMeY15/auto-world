the SDK's UTF-16 order in both stores.

verified HeadBucket absence only; authentication/transport errors remain failures.

behavior: a completed replay legitimately appends one fresh-fence `run.opened`
receipt/outbox event (all earlier receipts and business effects remain exact),
and recovery taking longer than 60 seconds legitimately returns `stale` without
`totalCount`. Recovery verifies every row, original `dataAsOf`, metadata, count
and uniqueness without resetting the clock, republishing data or extending the
freshness policy.
