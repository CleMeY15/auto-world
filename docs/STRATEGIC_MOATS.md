# Strategic Moats

Architecture must compound proprietary value over time instead of only shipping UI features.

## 1 Vehicle Graph
Track the same real-world vehicle across listings, sellers, countries and time. Preserve evidence and confidence.

## 2 Vehicle Knowledge Graph
Versioned global automotive taxonomy: make/model/generation/variant/engine/body/transmission/drivetrain/options/codes and regional naming aliases.

## 3 Historical Market Graph
Accumulate price, mileage, stock duration, source, seller and geography observations. Never discard useful historical observations merely because a listing disappears.

## 4 Provenance & Trust
Every critical fact is attributable. Contradictions become intelligence rather than destructive overwrites.

## 5 Pricing & Market Intelligence
Build transparent comparables, liquidity, depreciation and market indices from accumulated data.

## 6 Golden Data & Feedback
Human-reviewed datasets + user corrections + automated evals improve normalization, dedup, AI and pricing over time.

## 7 Direct Supply Network
Make it increasingly attractive for dealers/DMS/partners to push inventory directly. Build dealer APIs/feeds and analytics so coverage becomes less dependent on third-party marketplaces.

## 8 Purchase Intelligence
History, VIN, specs, emissions, French registration, import landed cost, Deal Score, Trust Score and compare make Auto World useful after discovery, not just before the click.

## Prioritization rule
When two implementations are equally viable, prefer the one that improves one or more compounding moats without materially harming simplicity, legal safety, cost or delivery speed.
